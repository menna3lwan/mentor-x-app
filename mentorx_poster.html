📄Abstract

The rapid digitization of education has exposed clear gaps in how learning is delivered, monitored, and supported. Existing platforms remain fragmented — focusing on either content delivery or basic LMS features — while leaving parents, teachers, and analytics disconnected.

MentorX is a unified, AI-powered educational ecosystem that brings students, parents, and teachers into a single intelligent platform. It integrates course management, automated attendance, secure assessments, gamified learning, real-time analytics, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) chatbot, and an OCR-based National ID verification system.

By combining a multi-tenant SaaS architecture with mobile-first design, data-driven insights, and modern security practices, MentorX transforms isolated digital tools into one connected learning experience — making education smarter, fairer, and more transparent.

⚠️Problem Statement

Traditional and existing e-learning platforms suffer from critical limitations:

🔻

Fragmented Ecosystems

Students, parents, and teachers operate in disconnected silos with no unified data layer.

🔻

Weak Parental Involvement

No real-time visibility into attendance, grades, or learning progress.

🔻

Manual Attendance & Grading

Error-prone, time-consuming, and unscalable for centers and online classes.

🔻

Limited Intelligence

Minimal AI for personalized guidance, predictions, or content recommendations.

🔻

Compromised Academic Integrity

Account sharing, impersonation, and unverified identities in online assessments.

🧠Intelligent AI Assistant

Two AI engines built for the Egyptian educational context.

RAG-grounded answers from teacher-uploaded materials and an OCR pipeline that authenticates Arabic National IDs at registration — combining accuracy, trust, and language fluency.

OpenAI GPT-4 LangChain YOLOv8 EasyOCR

🎓 RAG-Powered Chatbot

Retrieval-Augmented Generation grounded only in teacher-uploaded content. Produces curriculum-aligned answers, smart summaries, and practice question generation.

Stack: GPT-4 · LangChain · Vector DB · Embedding Models

🆔 National ID Verification

YOLOv8 + EasyOCR pipeline authenticating Arabic National IDs at registration with an 85%+ similarity threshold for auto-approval; manual review fallback.

Stack: Python · OpenCV · YOLOv8 · EasyOCR · RapidFuzz
