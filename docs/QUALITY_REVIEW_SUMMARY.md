### 2. Clarity and Readability Enhancements (5 corrections)

#### 2.1 Specific vs. Vague Language
- **Before:** "designed to **revolutionize** the educational experience for high school students"
- **After:** "designed to **transform** the educational experience for high school students **by integrating AI-powered tools**"
- **Rationale:** "Revolutionize" is overused and vague; added specific mechanism

#### 2.2 Punctuation for Clarity
- **Before:** "more than content delivery**—**it demands intelligent"
- **After:** "more than content delivery**: it demands intelligent"
- **Rationale:** Colon is clearer than em-dash for introducing explanation

#### 2.3 Terminology Clarity
- **Before:** "**Fourth Wave** (2020s-present)"
- **After:** "**Fourth Generation** (2020s-present)"
- **Rationale:** "Generation" is clearer and more professional than "Wave"
- **Instances:** Applied to First, Second, Third, Fourth, and Fifth Wave references

#### 2.4 Future Tense Clarity
- **Before:** "Planned improvements include:"
- **After:** "Planned improvements **under consideration** include:"
- **Rationale:** Clarifies these are potential, not committed, improvements

#### 2.5 Stronger Verb Choice
- **Before:** "**Maintained** academic integrity through"
- **After:** "**Ensured** academic integrity through"
- **Rationale:** "Ensured" is stronger and more active than "Maintained"

### 4. Passive to Active Voice Conversions (5 corrections)

All passive "shall be" constructions converted to active voice for clarity and directness:

#### 4.1 Logging Activities
- **Before:** "Unauthorized access attempts **shall be logged** and alerted"
- **After:** "**The system shall log** and alert unauthorized access attempts"

- **Before:** "Session activity **shall be logged** for audit purposes"
- **After:** "**The system shall log** session activity for audit purposes"

- **Before:** "Profile modifications **shall be validated and audit-logged**"
- **After:** "**The system shall validate and log** profile modifications"

#### 4.2 Encryption Operations
- **Before:** "Database connections **shall be encrypted**"
- **After:** "**The system shall encrypt** database connections"

- **Before:** "Messages **shall be encrypted** in transit and at rest"
- **After:** "**The system shall encrypt** messages in transit and at rest"

#### 4.3 Platform Capabilities
- **Before:** "Data portability **shall be supported** (JSON, CSV export)"
- **After:** "**The platform shall support** data portability (JSON, CSV export)"

#### 4.4 Data Storage
- **Before:** "Grades **shall be stored** in student profile"
- **After:** "**The system shall store** grades in student profile"

**Rationale:** Active voice is clearer, more direct, and assigns clear responsibility to system components.
