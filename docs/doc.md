1.  ***Introduction***

> ***MentorX** is designed as a strategic solution to enhance the
> learning experience and address the common challenges faced in modern
> education. The platform emphasizes active engagement, transparency,
> and intelligent support to create a more effective and motivating
> environment for students, parents, and teachers.*
>
> *One of the major challenges in education is student absence and the
> difficulty of managing makeup sessions. MentorX tackles this by
> providing an online system with structured makeup processes, ensuring
> that students do not miss valuable learning opportunities. Similarly,
> the lack of parent transparency is addressed through regular WhatsApp
> reports and a dedicated dashboard that provides real-time access to
> attendance, grades, and teacher notes.*
>
> *Revenue loss caused by session sharing is another significant issue.
> MentorX prevents this by implementing device-bound accounts, single IP
> restrictions, and OTP verification, ensuring both fairness for
> educators and sustainability for institutions. In addition, the
> widespread issue of exam cheating is mitigated by using AI-generated
> exam questions that vary in numbers and data, offering personalized
> assessments that maintain academic integrity.*
>
> *Finally**, the platform recognizes the overwhelming burden on
> teachers who manage large numbers of students**. MentorX provides a
> comprehensive teacher dashboard with complete student analytics,
> enabling educators to monitor progress efficiently and focus on
> individualized support.*
>
> *Through these innovations, MentorX not only solves recurring
> educational problems but also introduces long-term improvements in
> student engagement, parent involvement, institutional management, and
> overall learning outcomes.\*

2.  ***PROS AND CONS OF THE MENTORX APPROACH***

***1. Pros of the MentorX Approach***

*The MentorX platform presents a wide range of benefits that make the
educational process more dynamic, transparent, and efficient. A key
advantage lies in **ensuring learning continuity**: students who miss
classes can access structured makeup sessions online, minimizing
learning gaps and maximizing knowledge retention.*

*Another important strength is **parental involvement**. By integrating
WhatsApp reports and a parent dashboard, MentorX creates transparency,
allowing parents to track attendance, grades, and teacher feedback. This
level of communication builds trust and strengthens the student support
system.*

*MentorX also contributes to **academic integrity and fairness**.
Through AI-generated exam questions that vary in numbers and data, the
system reduces the likelihood of cheating while still ensuring that all
students are assessed on the same core content.*

*From an institutional perspective, MentorX safeguards against **revenue
loss and misuse** by restricting accounts to a single device and IP
address, with OTP verification for extra security. This not only
protects teachers' efforts but also enhances the sustainability of
educational centers.*

*Finally, the platform directly supports teachers by offering a
**comprehensive analytics dashboard**. This system reduces the burden of
tracking large groups of students manually and allows educators to focus
on providing more personalized learning support. In essence, MentorX
combines technology, transparency, and intelligent tools to foster a
supportive, motivating, and efficient learning environment.*

***2. Cons of the MentorX Approach***

*Despite its many advantages, the MentorX approach is not without its
challenges. One of the main limitations is its **dependence on
technology**. Since the system relies on online platforms, device-based
restrictions, and AI-powered tools, any technical issue such as poor
connectivity, device limitations, or software errors could interrupt the
learning process.*

*Another drawback lies in **resource requirements**. Implementing
dashboards, AI-driven assessments, and secure access systems may demand
financial investment, as well as training for both teachers and parents
to use the platform effectively. Smaller institutions may face barriers
in adopting such systems.*

*There is also the potential issue of **student adaptability**. While
gamification and online learning tools are engaging for many, some
students may struggle with the transition from traditional methods,
leading to unequal participation or varying levels of comfort with the
system.*

*Finally, ensuring **data security and privacy** is an ongoing
challenge. With the integration of parent dashboards, online exams, and
student performance tracking, institutions must ensure that sensitive
educational data is protected against misuse or breaches.*

3.  ***PROBLEMS AND SOLUTIONS***

*The education sector faces a variety of recurring challenges that
affect students, parents, and teachers alike. MentorX was designed to
address these issues systematically by providing clear,
technology-driven solutions that enhance the overall learning
environment. Below, we outline the major problems and the corresponding
solutions offered by MentorX.*

***1. Student Absence and Makeup Sessions***

***Problem:**\
Students often miss classes due to personal, health, or scheduling
reasons. In traditional educational systems, there is no structured way
to compensate for these missed lessons, leaving gaps in understanding
and hindering academic progress.*

***Solution:**\
MentorX provides an **online session system** with a structured makeup
process. This ensures that students who miss classes can access recorded
sessions, summaries, assignments, and quizzes. In doing so, it minimizes
learning gaps and guarantees that no student is left behind.*

***2. Lack of Parent Transparency***

***Problem:**\
Parents struggle to monitor their children's academic performance due to
the absence of a clear reporting system. This lack of visibility leads
to weak communication between parents and instructors, making it
difficult to intervene when students underperform.*

***Solution:**\
MentorX introduces a **parent dashboard** supported by WhatsApp updates.
This system provides instant access to attendance records, grades,
teacher feedback, and performance summaries. By keeping parents
informed, MentorX strengthens parental involvement and ensures better
academic support for students.*

***3. Session Sharing and Revenue Loss***

***Problem:**\
In online education, students often share access to paid sessions with
peers. This not only reduces institutional revenue but also wastes
teacher effort and disrupts the fairness of the learning process.*

***Solution:**\
MentorX employs a **security-focused account system**. Each student
account is restricted to a single device and IP address, verified
through OTP (One-Time Password). These measures prevent unauthorized
sharing and secure the financial sustainability of educational
institutions.*

***4. Exam Cheating***

***Problem:**\
Traditional exams often use identical question sets for all students,
making it easy for learners to share answers. This practice undermines
academic integrity and fails to provide an accurate assessment of
student performance.*

***Solution:**\
MentorX integrates **AI-generated question banks**. These ensure that
while the core concepts remain the same, the numerical values and data
points vary from one student to another. As a result, every student
receives a personalized exam, minimizing cheating opportunities and
enhancing the credibility of assessments.*

***5. Teacher Overwhelm***

***Problem:**\
Teachers are responsible for managing hundreds of students, making it
nearly impossible to monitor individual performance consistently. This
leads to oversight, inefficiencies, and teacher burnout.*

***Solution:**\
MentorX offers a **comprehensive teacher dashboard** with complete
student analytics. This platform enables instructors to track
attendance, grades, and participation in real time. With automated
reports and intelligent analytics, teachers can focus on personalized
guidance instead of administrative overload.*

4.  ***OBJECTIVES***

*The primary objectives of MentorX revolve around transforming the
learning process into a more accessible, transparent, and engaging
experience for all stakeholders---students, parents, teachers, and
institutions. By integrating technology and intelligent tools, MentorX
aims to resolve persistent challenges in education and create
sustainable long-term improvements. The following key objectives define
the vision and mission of the platform:*

***1. Enhancing the Learning Experience***

> *One of the foremost objectives of MentorX is to ensure that students
> never miss a learning opportunity, regardless of whether they attend
> in person or online. The platform provides recorded sessions, lesson
> summaries, assignments, and quizzes. These resources allow learners to
> revise material at their own pace, reinforce understanding, and bridge
> any gaps caused by absence. This approach ensures continuity in
> learning and promotes independent study skills.*

***2. Improving Parent Involvement***

> *Parental engagement is a critical factor in student success. MentorX
> addresses the lack of transparency between institutions and families
> by offering **regular reports and real-time notifications**. Parents
> receive updates on attendance, grades, assignments, and teacher
> feedback through WhatsApp and a dedicated parent dashboard. This
> system ensures that parents remain active partners in their children's
> education and can provide timely support when needed.*

***3. Increasing Student Motivation through Gamification***

> *Motivation is a cornerstone of effective learning. MentorX
> incorporates **gamification elements** such as points, rewards, and
> badges, encouraging students to stay engaged and consistent in their
> academic efforts. By linking rewards to both attendance and
> performance, the platform fosters healthy competition,
> self-motivation, and a sense of achievement among learners.*

***4. Integrated Center Management***

> *Beyond the student and parent experience, MentorX also focuses on
> improving operational efficiency for educational institutions. The
> system introduces **integrated management tools**, including QR
> code--based attendance tracking. This not only reduces errors and time
> consumption but also enhances reliability in administrative processes,
> making center operations more seamless and effective.*

***5. Intelligent Learning Support with AI***

> *The integration of **artificial intelligence** is a distinctive
> feature of MentorX. The platform includes an AI-powered assistant that
> functions as a virtual tutor, providing personalized academic support.
> This assistant can explain difficult topics, generate additional
> practice questions, suggest customized study plans, and even predict
> exam results. By offering adaptive learning paths, the AI component
> ensures that each student receives tailored guidance aligned with
> their individual needs and pace.*

5.  ***F******unctional & NON-REQUIREMENTS***

- ***Functional requirements***

> *The functional requirements specify all system capabilities and
> behaviors that are essential for the successful operation of the
> MentorX platform. These requirements describe how each component
> should function to support students, instructors, parents,
> administrators, and the AI learning engine.*
>
> ***1.1.1 User Management***
>
> ***1. User Registration***
>
> *The system shall permit users (students, instructors, parents, and
> administrators) to securely create new accounts through a registration
> interface accessible via web and mobile platforms.\
> Each registration attempt shall require mandatory input fields
> including:*

- *Full name*

- *Email address*

- *Mobile number*

- *User role selection (student, teacher, parent, or admin)*

- *Secure password*

> *The system shall perform input validation to ensure:*

- *The email address conforms to RFC 5322 standard format.*

- *The password meets complexity requirements (minimum 8 characters,
  alphanumeric, at least one special character).*

> *The system shall send a **One-Time Password (OTP)** via both email
> and SMS to validate the user's identity before activation.\
> Additionally, the platform shall bind each account to a unique device
> identifier (IMEI) and IP address to prevent credential sharing or
> unauthorized access.\
> If a user attempts to access the platform from an unrecognized device,
> a secondary OTP verification or administrator approval shall be
> required.*
>
> ***2. Secure Authentication***
>
> *The system shall implement a **multi-layered authentication
> mechanism** consisting of:*

1.  *Username/Email and Password verification.*

2.  *OTP or two-factor authentication (2FA).*

3.  *Device and IP recognition.*

> *All credentials shall be transmitted securely using **TLS 1.3
> encryption**.\
> Failed login attempts exceeding three (3) consecutive tries shall
> result in temporary account suspension and a security alert
> notification.\
> The system shall automatically log users out after a configurable
> period of inactivity to prevent session hijacking.*
>
> ***3. User Profile Management***
>
> *Each user shall have access to a **Profile Management Interface**
> where personal details such as name, photo, contact information, and
> password can be updated.\
> Profile modifications shall be validated and recorded in audit logs
> for traceability.\
> Parents shall be permitted to link multiple student profiles under
> their account for consolidated monitoring.\
> All updates shall propagate across dependent modules, ensuring data
> consistency in dashboards, reports, and analytics.*
>
> ***1.1.2 Course and Content Management***
>
> ***4. Course Creation***
>
> *The system shall provide instructors with a **Course Management
> Dashboard** for creating and configuring courses.\
> Each new course shall require input fields including course title,
> description, subject, grade level, duration, schedule, and pricing (if
> applicable).\
> Instructors shall be able to attach course materials such as:*

- *PDF documents*

- *PowerPoint files*

- *Recorded video lectures*

- *Interactive links*

> *Upon submission, the system shall automatically verify file types and
> generate metadata for indexing and search optimization.*
>
> ***5. Course Editing and Deletion***
>
> *Instructors shall have the authority to modify course content, update
> schedules, or deactivate outdated materials.\
> Deletion requests shall require confirmation and administrative
> approval when enrolled students are affected.\
> All deleted courses shall be archived within a **Course Repository**
> for future restoration or audit reference.*
>
> ***6. Lesson Management***
>
> *Each course shall contain a sequence of lessons managed through a
> dedicated interface.\
> Lessons shall include:*

- *Title and description*

- *Video or live session link*

- *Summary notes and supporting documents*

- *Assignments or practice tasks*

> *The system shall enforce **lesson progression control**, ensuring
> students cannot access subsequent lessons without completing
> prerequisites (e.g., watching videos, submitting assignments, or
> passing a quiz).\
> Completion status shall be automatically updated in the student's
> progress record.*
>
> ***1.1.3 Attendance and Engagement Tracking***
>
> ***7. Attendance Recording***
>
> *For physical sessions, students shall record attendance via **QR code
> scanning** generated by the system and linked to their unique Student
> ID.\
> For online sessions, attendance shall be automatically logged when
> students join live classes, interact with materials, or submit
> assignments.\
> In cases of absence, automated notifications shall be sent to the
> parent and teacher dashboard.\
> Attendance logs shall be stored in the database for at least one
> academic year and accessible for reporting.*
>
> ***8. Engagement Analytics***
>
> *The system shall track student engagement metrics, including:*

- *Video completion rates*

- *Assignment submission frequency*

- *Login activity*

- *Session duration*

> *AI algorithms shall process this data to determine participation
> levels and detect early signs of disengagement or dropout risk.*
>
> ***1.1.4 Assignments and Examinations***
>
> ***9. Assignment Management***
>
> *Instructors shall upload assignments linked to specific lessons or
> topics.\
> Students shall submit assignments via file upload, structured forms,
> or text input.\
> The system shall timestamp every submission and flag late entries.\
> AI models shall automatically grade objective questions and provide
> instructors with suggestions for subjective answer evaluation.*
>
> ***10. Exam Creation and Randomization***
>
> *Instructors shall create exams by specifying parameters including:*

- *Course and topic coverage*

- *Number of questions*

- *Difficulty level (easy, medium, hard)*

- *Exam duration*

- *Grading weightage*

> *An integrated **AI Question Generator** shall automatically create
> randomized and unique question variations per student to prevent
> cheating.\
> The generated questions shall be validated against the course content
> database to ensure relevance and accuracy.*
>
> ***11. Exam Administration***
>
> *Students shall take exams within a **secure, monitored environment**
> that:*

- *Disables navigation away from the exam page.*

- *Includes real-time terms and progress tracking.*

- *Saves answers periodically (auto-save every 30 seconds).*

- *Prevents multiple logins during an exam session.*

> *Upon submission, AI algorithms shall instantly grade objective
> questions, while subjective responses shall be queued for instructor
> review.*
>
> ***12. Grading and Feedback***
>
> *Grading shall be performed through a hybrid model:*

- *Automated AI grading for structured questions.*

- *Instructor grading for essay-type responses.*

> *The system shall calculate final scores, store them in the student
> profile, and notify both student and parent upon release.\
> Teachers shall be able to modify scores manually, with reasons
> documented in audit logs.\
> Students shall receive detailed performance feedback, highlighting
> strong and weak areas.*
>
> ***1.1.5 Communication and Collaboration***
>
> ***13. Public Discussion Forums***
>
> *Each course and lesson shall include a moderated discussion board.\
> Students and teachers may post questions, replies, and resource
> links.\
> An AI-based moderation filter shall detect and flag offensive or
> irrelevant content.\
> All discussions shall be stored for academic tracking and future
> reference.*
>
> ***14. Private Messaging***
>
> *The platform shall include an internal messaging system that enables
> direct communication between:*

- *Student ↔ Teacher*

- *Parent ↔ Teacher*

- *Admin ↔ User*

> *Unread messages shall trigger notification badges and optional email
> alerts.\
> Message data shall be encrypted and retained according to privacy
> policy settings.*
>
> ***15. AI Chatbot Assistance***
>
> *An AI chatbot shall be integrated across all modules to:*

- *Provide real-time help and guidance.*

- *Answer frequently asked questions.*

- *Recommend learning materials.*

- *Offer troubleshooting support.*

> *The chatbot shall use Natural Language Processing (NLP) for
> contextual understanding and continuous learning based on user
> interactions.*
>
> ***1.1.6 Financial and Administrative Operations***
>
> ***16. Payment Management***
>
> *The system shall manage all financial transactions related to:*

- *Tuition fees*

- *Course subscriptions*

- *Refund requests*

- *Promotional discounts*

> *Payments shall be processed through secure gateways (e.g., PayPal,
> Stripe, or Fawry).\
> Admins shall have access to a **Financial Dashboard** for tracking
> revenue, expenses, and overdue payments.*
>
> ***17. Reporting and Analytics***
>
> *Administrators shall generate analytical reports including:*

- *Student performance summaries*

- *Teacher productivity metrics*

- *Revenue reports*

- *Attendance statistics*

- *Dropout risk predictions*

> *Reports shall be exportable in PDF, Excel, or CSV formats and
> accessible by authorized personnel only.*
>
> ***1.1.7 Security and Access Control***
>
> ***18. Access Levels***
>
> *The system shall implement **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** to
> restrict functionality based on user type:*

- *Students: access only to personal academic data.*

- *Teachers: access to managed courses and enrolled students.*

- *Parents: access to child-related data.*

- *Admins: full system oversight and configuration authority.*

> ***19. Session Management***
>
> *All user sessions shall have unique tokens with expiration
> timestamps.\
> The system shall immediately terminate sessions upon logout or
> security breach detection.*
>
> ***1.2 NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS***
>
> *The non-functional requirements define system quality attributes such
> as performance, scalability, reliability, and compliance that govern
> the overall user experience and platform robustness.*
>
> ***2.2.1 Performance***

- *The system shall support a minimum of **10,000 concurrent active
  users** with no degradation in response time.*

- *Average server response time shall not exceed **2 seconds** for 95%
  of transactions.*

- *The platform shall deliver real-time updates (attendance, grades,
  notifications) with a maximum delay of **1 second**.*

- *Database queries shall be optimized to maintain read/write operations
  within **300 milliseconds**.*

> ***2.2.2 Scalability***

- *The architecture shall support **horizontal scaling** of backend
  services.*

- *Auto-scaling shall be configured on cloud infrastructure (AWS/Google
  Cloud) to handle traffic spikes.*

- *System modules (AI, backend, storage) shall be containerized for
  efficient deployment and scaling.*

> ***2.2.3 Reliability and Availability***

- *The system shall maintain **99.5% uptime** annually.*

- *Redundant servers shall ensure continuous operation during
  maintenance or server failure.*

- *Automatic failover and data recovery mechanisms shall be implemented
  to prevent service disruption.*

- *Data backup frequency shall be **every 24 hours** with retention for
  **90 days**.*

> ***2.2.4 Security***

- *All communication shall be encrypted using **SSL/TLS (HTTPS)**.*

- *Sensitive data shall be encrypted at rest using **AES-256**.*

- *Multi-factor authentication (MFA) shall be mandatory for all
  privileged accounts.*

- *The system shall be resistant to common web vulnerabilities (SQL
  Injection, XSS, CSRF).*

- *Regular penetration testing shall be conducted every quarter.*

> ***2.2.5 Usability***

- *The user interface shall adhere to **WCAG 2.1 accessibility
  standards**, ensuring support for visually impaired users.*

- *Font sizes, contrast ratios, and layout responsiveness shall be
  consistent across devices.*

- *Interactive tutorials and help sections shall guide first-time
  users.*

- *Navigation flow shall remain uniform across dashboards.*

> ***2.2.6 Maintainability***

- *The codebase shall adopt modular and microservices architecture.*

- *Continuous Integration and Continuous Deployment (CI/CD) pipelines
  shall automate testing and deployment.*

- *Source code shall be version-controlled using GitHub or GitLab.*

- *Documentation shall accompany all APIs, database schemas, and system
  modules.*

> ***2.2.7 Compatibility***

- *The platform shall support all major browsers: Chrome, Edge, Firefox,
  and Safari.*

- *The mobile application shall support Android (v10+) and iOS (v14+).*

- *Backend APIs shall follow RESTful conventions and JSON standards.*

> ***2.2.8 Privacy and Compliance***

- *The system shall comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA
  equivalents).*

- *Users shall be informed of data collection and consent policies upon
  registration.*

- *The system shall support data anonymization and deletion requests.*

- *Audit logs shall maintain a record of all access, modification, and
  deletion events.*

> ***2.2.9 Accessibility***

- *The platform shall integrate **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** and
  **Speech-to-Text (STT)** for inclusivity.*

- *Voice navigation and dictation features shall be available on both

*RAG Chatbot*

# *Title*

***Retrieval**‑**Augmented Generation (RAG) Educational Chatbot for
High**‑**School Teacher***

# *Abstract*

*This report describes the design, implementation and evaluation of a
knowledge‑grounded educational chatbot for a single high‑school teacher
and one subject. The chatbot uses a RAG pipeline: document ingestion →
chunking → embeddings → vector DB → semantic retrieval → LLM generation.
The goal is a practical, deployable prototype (Streamlit) that answers
students' questions based on the teacher's real material and achieves
measurable accuracy on a held‑out QA set.*

# *Goals*

- *Build a chatbot that answers student questions from the teacher's
  materials (PDFs, slides, notes).*

- *Keep the teacher's voice and factual correctness (avoid
  hallucination) by conditioning generation on retrieved text.*

- *Produce a demo UI and evaluate quality (similarity/accuracy) on a
  test set (e.g. 50--200 Qs).*

# *System Overview (Input → Output)*

***Input:** free‑text student question (Arabic)*

***Preprocessing:** normalize, remove punctuation, optional Arabic
diacritics removal, tokenize if needed.*

***Embedding:** convert cleaned question to vector via
SentenceTransformer (or OpenAI embeddings).*

***Retrieval:** compute cosine similarity against stored vectors
(chunks). Return top‑k chunks.*

***Answer Generation:** construct a prompt that includes retrieved
chunks and the student question; send to LLM to generate a concise
answer restricted to the retrieved context*

***Postprocessing:** truncate, apply simple rules (credit the source,
add references, confidence score).*

***Output:** answer text + confidence + source links (lesson titles /
paragraph IDs).*

# *Data (What to feed the system)*

1.  *Teacher's canonical materials: PDFs, DOCX, PPTX, lecture scripts.*

2.  *FAQ / historical student questions (if available).*

3.  *Book excerpts (optional) --- cite sources.*

4.  *Metadata: lesson IDs, timestamps, video links.*

***Storage format for knowledge:** a table / JSON with columns:
chunk_id, lesson_id, chunk_text, embedding_vector, source_path,
start_pos, end_pos.*

# *Preprocessing & Chunking*

- *Extract text from PDF/DOCX/PPTX (PyMuPDF / pdfminer / python-docx /
  python-pptx).*

- *Clean text: normalize Arabic characters ([ى]{dir="rtl"} ←
  [ي]{dir="rtl"} ,[ا]{dir="rtl"} → [أ]{dir="rtl"} handling), remove
  non‑text garbage.*

- *Split text into chunks: recommended 200--600 tokens (≈250--500 words)
  with overlap 50--100 tokens.*

- *Keep chunk metadata: lesson title, page number, original filename.*

***Why chunking?** - Embedding models and retrieval work better on
semantically coherent passages. Overlap reduces lost context.*

# *Embeddings*

- *Local option: sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (fast, 384
  dims). Good for prototyping.*

- *Higher quality option: paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM or*

> *paraphrase-multilingual-mpnet-base-v2 for Arabic sensitivity.*

- *Cloud option: OpenAI text-embedding-3-small or
  text-embedding-3-large.*

### *Code sketch (Python)*

***from** sentence_transformers **import** SentenceTransformer model =
SentenceTransformer(\'all-MiniLM-L6-v2\')*

*embs = model.encode(list_of_chunks, show_progress_bar=True)*

# *Vector Database / Index*

*Options: - **FAISS** (local, fast) --- good for demo. Use IndexFlatIP
with normalized vectors for cosine similarity. - **ChromaDB** --- easy,
built for embeddings & small projects. - **Pinecone / Weaviate /
Milvus** --- production ready, scalable, managed.*

***Indexing steps:** 1. Normalize embeddings (L2 norm) for cosine by
inner product. 2. Build FAISS index with vectors and store mapping from
index→chunk metadata.*

# *Retrieval*

- *Query embedding → search top K (K=3--5).*

- *Return chunks sorted by score.*

- *Optionally apply a filtering layer: prefer recent lessons or same
  lesson type.*

# *Prompt Engineering & Generation*

### *Prompt pattern (conservative):*

*You are an educational assistant. Use only the following passages from
the teacher\'s material to answer the student\'s question. If the answer
is not found in the passages, say \"I don\'t find the answer in the
provided material. Please ask your teacher.\" Keep the reply short and
in Arabic.*

*Passages:*

1)  *\<chunk 1 text\>*

2)  *\<chunk 2 text\>*

*\...*

*Question: \<student question\> Answer:*

***LLM choices:** - OpenAI GPT‑4/3.5 (via API) --- best quality. - Local
Llama/Mistral-based models (if GPU available) --- offline option.*

***Safety trick to reduce hallucination:** instruct LLM to answer only
from the passages and to include the source ID for each factual
sentence.*

# *Confidence & Answer Verification*

- *Use retrieval scores to compute a confidence baseline (e.g., weighted
  similarity sum).*

- *Optionally re‑encode the generated answer and compute similarity to
  the retrieved passages to detect divergence.*

# *Evaluation (Accuracy / Metrics)*

***Data:** create a test set of Q→gold answer pairs (50--200 items). Use
teacher‑verified answers.*

***Automatic metrics:** - **Cosine similarity** between final answer
embedding and gold answer embedding (semantic similarity). - **BLEU /
ROUGE / Meteor** for lexical overlap (less ideal for Arabic). -
**BERTScore** (better semantic metric) or Sentence‑Transformers cosine.*

***Human evaluation:** - Grade answers as Correct / Partially /
Incorrect. - Collect teacher feedback: Is the answer pedagogically
correct? Is phrasing appropriate?*

***Reporting:** show average similarity, %Correct, confusion examples.*

# *Implementation Plan (Code + Demo)*

***Minimal prototype (for the defence/demo):** 1. Scripts: - ingest.py
--- extract + chunk*

*+ embed + index (FAISS). - server.py --- FastAPI endpoint: /ask accepts
question, returns answer+sources+confidence. - app.py --- Streamlit demo
UI calling /ask. 2. Local stack: Python, sentence-transformers,
faiss-cpu, transformers, openai (optional), fastapi, uvicorn,
streamlit.*

***Evaluation Metrics***

*Since this is a multi-class classification problem where the classes
(grades A, B, C, D, F) are inherently imbalanced, the following metrics
were tracked, with a focus on weighted averaging to account for class
imbalance:*

***Accuracy:** Overall correct predictions {Accuracy} = {Correct
Predictions}/ {Total Samples}*

***Precision (Weighted):** The ratio of correctly predicted positive
observations to the total predicted positive observations, averaged
across all classes and weighted by support.*

***Recall (Weighted):** The ratio of correctly predicted positive
observations to all observations in the actual class, averaged across
all classes and weighted by support.*

***F1-Score (Weighted):** The harmonic mean of Precision and Recall.
This is the **primary metric** used to select the best model, as it
balances the trade-off between Precision and Recall across all grade
classes. The model with the highest **Test F1-Score** is considered the
best.*
